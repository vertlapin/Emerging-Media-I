Bio

Ara was born and raised in Humacao, Puerto Rico, and moved to New York City in 2024, on the lookout for new opportunities. Her experience in film and television includes working in the costume departments of The Resort (Peacock) and Fantasy Island (Hulu), where she worked as a background costumer and buyer. Her creative practice also includes screenwriting. In 2027, she will participate in a screenwriting residency in Vermont, where she will continue developing her screenplay Miss Texas through mentorship at Stowe. She is currently the owner and curator of Pêche d’Été, a small shop at 2 Bridge Mall in Chinatown specializing in antique, vintage, and designer clothing. Through the shop, she creates an immersive shopping experience shaped by lighting, sound, and spatial design.
