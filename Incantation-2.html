Incantation II

Project Abstract

Chatroom is an online chat and interactive website inspired by the old-school anonymous internet chatrooms I remember using as a teenager, particularly ICQ. The project explores nostalgia, anonymity, identity, and the excitement of meeting strangers online, before social media profiles, dating apps, and AI companions became part of our everyday lives. Through photography, a poster, and an interactive website, I want to revisit a time when people could enter a chatroom, invent an identity, have an unexpected conversation, and disappear without leaving much behind. Visitors will be invited to choose a username or receive a randomly generated one, enter different chatrooms, and decide how much of themselves they want to reveal. They can also privately message someone they find interesting.
